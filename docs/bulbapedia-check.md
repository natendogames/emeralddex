## Review notes

### Mismatches
- route-118: Gabby & Ty: the app lists their first battle on Route 111 (data/scripts/gabby_and_ty.inc); the Route 118 and 120 battles are rematches. OK.
- route-120: Gabby & Ty rematch, see Route 118. OK.
- mossdeep-city: Bulbapedia puts the Wingull boy's Mental Herb under Mossdeep; the decomp gives it in FortreeCity_House4 (the boy's Wingull carries mail to Mossdeep). App is right.
- dewford-town: Battle Girl Shauna/Jocelyn is one trainer with a hover name on Bulbapedia; matched as Jocelyn. OK.

### Story order
- route-105: App lists Routes 105-108 where you can Surf them; Bulbapedia mentions them first on Mr. Briney's boat ride. By design.
- route-118: App lists Route 118 when you can Surf across it; Bulbapedia mentions the west bank earlier. By design.
- lilycove-city: Safari Zone needs the Pokéblock Case from Lilycove. Added a step saying so.
- route-123: Optional detour; either order works.
- route-132: Optional currents after Pacifidlog; either order works.
- desert-underpass: Post-game optional areas; order is free.
- sealed-chamber: Post-game optional areas; order is free.
- desert-ruins: Post-game optional areas; order is free.

### Not covered
Bulbapedia's Emerald Part 22 (Southern Island, Faraway Island, Birth Island, Navel Rock) doesn't exist on the wiki, so those four event islands stay Draft.

### Corrections made to the app
- Hiker Mike in Rusturf Tunnel was missing: build_data.py treated TRAINER_MIKE_2 as a rematch, but MIKE_1 is never battled.
- Gabby & Ty were missing: their battles live in data/scripts/gabby_and_ty.inc, which the map pass didn't read. Now on Route 111.
- TM43 (Secret Power) gift on Route 111 was missing (shared script data/scripts/secret_power_tm.inc).
- Rayquaza is catchable as soon as it returns to Sky Pillar after the Sootopolis event (VAR_SKY_PILLAR_STATE >= 2), not only after the Hall of Fame.
- Sky Pillar: the first climb needs no bike; the Mach Bike is needed when you come back for Rayquaza.
- Cave of Origin: Wallace waits on B1F, and Zubat, Golbat and Sableye live there (the old step said nothing could be caught).
- Route 123: one-way ledges run west to Route 118; the Giga Drain girl checks for a Grass-type in your party, not a Grass move.
- Route 132: the currents are west of Pacifidlog, not east.
- Route 115: you can walk there north from Rustboro.
- Verdanturf: Wally is staying with his cousin Wanda's family.
- Desert Underpass: reached through the Fossil Maniac's cave on Route 114.
- Safari Zone: needs the Pokéblock Case; the extra areas open with the National Pokédex.
